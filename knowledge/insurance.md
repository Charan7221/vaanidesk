# Insurance and reimbursement

## Cashless treatment
Sunrise Family Clinic is an outpatient clinic and does not offer cashless insurance or TPA billing.
Patients pay at the clinic and can claim the amount from their insurer if their policy covers outpatient (OPD) care.

## Documents for claims
For an insurance or company reimbursement claim, ask the reception for:
- an itemised bill with the clinic stamp,
- a copy of the prescription signed by the doctor,
- lab reports, if tests were done at the clinic.
These are provided free of charge, usually the same day.

## Government health schemes
The clinic is not empanelled under Aarogyasri or Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY). Patients needing those schemes are
referred to the District Hospital, Vizianagaram.
