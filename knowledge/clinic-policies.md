# Clinic policies

## Appointments and waiting
Patients with appointments are seen first. Walk-in patients are seen between appointments, so waiting can be longer.
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to complete registration.
If you are more than 15 minutes late, your slot may be given to the next patient and you will be seen as a walk-in.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
Please cancel or change your appointment at least 2 hours before the time by calling the clinic.
There is no cancellation fee.

## What to bring
Bring any old prescriptions, recent test reports and the list of medicines you are taking.

## Visitors
To keep the waiting area safe for everyone, one attendant is allowed with each patient.

## Home visits and online consultations
Home visits are not available. Video consultations for follow-ups can be arranged with the doctor's approval;
ask the reception.

## Emergencies
The clinic does not handle emergencies. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, severe bleeding
or accidents, call 108 immediately or go to the District Hospital emergency department.

## Location and facilities
Address: 12-4-56, MG Road, Vizianagaram, Andhra Pradesh, opposite the State Bank of India branch.
Free parking for two-wheelers and cars is available in front of the clinic. The clinic is on the ground floor
and is wheelchair accessible.
