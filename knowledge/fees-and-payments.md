# Fees and payments

## Consultation fees
- General consultation with Dr. Srinivasa Rao (General Physician): ₹400.
- Child consultation with Dr. Lakshmi Devi (Paediatrician): ₹450.
- Follow-up visit for the same problem within 7 days of the first visit: free. Please bring the earlier prescription.
- Senior citizens (60 years and above) get 10% off the consultation fee. Please show any age proof.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and any UPI app) and all major debit and credit cards.
Payment is collected at the reception desk before the consultation.

## Receipts and bills
A printed receipt is given for every payment. An itemised bill with the doctor's registration number
can be printed on request at no extra cost, for example for office reimbursement.
