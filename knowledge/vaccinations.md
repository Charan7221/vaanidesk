# Child vaccinations

## Vaccination days
Children's vaccinations are given by Dr. Lakshmi Devi on Tuesday and Saturday mornings, 9 AM to 12 noon.
Please book an appointment so the vaccine can be kept ready.

## What to bring
Bring the child's vaccination card or booklet and the birth certificate for the first visit.
The child should be well on the day; if the child has high fever, call the clinic to move the appointment.

## Vaccine availability and cost
The clinic follows the Indian Academy of Paediatrics (IAP) schedule and stocks the routine childhood vaccines.
Prices depend on the vaccine and brand; the reception can share the current price list on WhatsApp.
Free government vaccines are given at the nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC), not at this clinic.
