# Laboratory and tests

## Sample collection timings
Blood and urine samples are collected at the clinic from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM, Monday to Saturday.
No appointment is needed for sample collection.

## Preparing for a test
- Fasting blood sugar and lipid profile: do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before the test. Plain water is allowed.
- Post-lunch blood sugar (PPBS): give the sample exactly 2 hours after starting lunch.
- Thyroid (TSH) and complete blood count (CBP): no fasting needed.
- Urine test: bring the first urine sample of the morning in the clean container given by the clinic.
Take your regular medicines as usual unless the doctor told you otherwise.

## Reports
Most reports are ready the same evening after 5 PM. Thyroid and HbA1c reports take 24 hours.
Reports are sent on WhatsApp to the registered mobile number and printed copies can be collected at the reception.

## Test prices
- Complete blood count (CBP): ₹250
- Fasting or random blood sugar: ₹80
- HbA1c: ₹450
- Lipid profile: ₹500
- Thyroid profile (T3, T4, TSH): ₹550
- Urine routine: ₹120
Tests not listed here, such as X-ray, ultrasound, CT or MRI, are not available at the clinic.
The doctor will refer you to a nearby diagnostic centre if needed.
