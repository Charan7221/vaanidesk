# Clinic knowledge base

Documents in this folder are indexed at startup and searched by the AI receptionist
(`search_clinic_knowledge` tool) to answer callers' questions, with the source shown on the dashboard.

Supported: `.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`. Headings (`#`, `##`) become the section names shown as citations.
You can also upload documents from the dashboard's **Knowledge** page.
These sample documents describe the fictional Sunrise Family Clinic. Replace them with your clinic's own.
