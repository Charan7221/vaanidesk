# OPD (outpatient department)

## OPD timings
Both doctors hold OPD every weekday in two sessions: morning 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and evening 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
On Saturday there is only the morning OPD (9:00 AM to 1:00 PM). There is no OPD on Sunday.
Dr. Srinivasa Rao (General Physician) sees adults and children above 14 years. Dr. Lakshmi Devi (Paediatrician)
sees children up to 14 years.

## How OPD tokens work
Every OPD session has its own token queue for each doctor. Patients are seen in token order, and patients
with a fixed appointment time may be seen between tokens.
You can take a token by calling the clinic (our receptionist gives it on the phone) for today or the next two days,
or at the reception desk on arrival. Phone tokens stop 30 minutes before the session ends; the desk gives walk-in
tokens until the session ends.
Each consultation takes about 10 minutes, so the expected time for your token is only an estimate.
If you miss your turn, tell the reception when you arrive and you will be seen after the next patient.

## OPD registration
First-time patients are registered at the reception and get a patient number, which is used for all later
visits and reports. Please bring a photo ID (Aadhaar, voter ID or driving licence) for the first visit, and
your patient number or token SMS on later visits.
Registration is free. Children are registered under a parent's mobile number.
